What's real in this mockup, and what isn't
  • Real: all 8 Day 3 stops, their times and drive legs, straight from your PDF.
  • Real: altitudes — Tbilisi 450 m climbing to Jvari Pass at 2,395 m, then down to Stepantsminda at 1,740 m.
  • Real: the food picks, from the research pass — Chabarukhi in Pasanauri, Tiba and Kazbegi Good Food up in Stepantsminda.
  • Real: photos are live from Wikimedia Commons with their actual credits and licences. In the built app they're downloaded, compressed and bundled so they work offline.
  • Placeholder: weather numbers are samples — the real app fetches them from Open-Meteo and caches them.
  • Placeholder: the mini-maps are stylised panels. The real one is MapLibre on an offline Georgia vector basemap with your car tracked live.
  • Placeholder: Days 1–2 and 4–9 are Phase 1 work. Only Day 3 is built here.
  • Coordinates shown are approximate; every one gets verified against OpenStreetMap in Phase 1.
